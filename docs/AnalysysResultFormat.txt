4. What causes users to repeatedly listen to the same content?

Here are 4 findings that explain why users repeatedly listen to the same content:

1. (Single-review finding) Users experience repetition loops when using the DJ feature.

Supporting evidence: Review 3 [App Store]: "When I use the dj it just plays the same stuff If I wanted to hear my liked sounds I would select my liked songs playlist"

Why it matters: This behavior can lead to a frustrating experience for users who expect a more dynamic playlist.

2. Users encounter issues with playlist recycling due to the auto-shuffle feature not working correctly.

Supporting evidence: Review 78 [Play Store]: "My playlists are also on auto shuffle but it doesn't even work right."

Review 69 [Play Store]: "If I click on a playlist it'll play a song or two then just start playing random music. It completely forgets what you were playing before."

Why it matters: This can cause users to repeatedly listen to the same content, as the playlist does not refresh as expected.

3. Smart shuffle can ruin custom playlists, leading to repetition.

Supporting evidence: Review 58 [Play Store]: "When I enable smart shuffle it ruins my custom playlists."

Why it matters: Users who rely on custom playlists may experience frustration when smart shuffle disrupts their carefully curated playlists.

4. Users experience repetition due to the lack of a dislike button, which can lead to unwanted songs appearing in playlists.

Supporting evidence: Review 55 [Play Store]: "I wish they would add a dislike button to compliment the heart. Hiding songs isn't the same. It doesn't solve the problem of a not liked song showing up on playlists."

Why it matters: Without a dislike button, users may be forced to listen to songs they don't enjoy, leading to repetition.

Product Opportunities
Question 6
What unmet needs emerge consistently across reviews?
Here are the unmet needs that emerge consistently across reviews:

1. Lack of control over skip functionality: Users feel restricted by the limited skips allowed, with some experiencing frustration when ads interrupt their listening experience.

Supporting evidence: "You can only skip 6 songs a day if you don't want to listen to them in sequence." (Review 72), "cant skip without long ads" (Review 37)

Why it matters: Users value the ability to skip tracks without interruptions, which is essential for a seamless listening experience.

2. Inadequate ad management: Users express frustration with the prevalence of ads, which can be intrusive and disrupt their listening experience.

Supporting evidence: "ads are everywhere" (Review 43), "constant unneeded ads" (Review 35)

Why it matters: Users want to enjoy their music without being interrupted by ads, which can be annoying and detract from their experience.

3. Limited playlist customization: Users feel restricted by the lack of control over playlist settings, including the inability to create playlists without shuffle mode.

Supporting evidence: "You can't even choose the songs you want to listen to" (Review 43), "cant make a playlist where its not on shuffle" (Review 43)

Why it matters: Users value the ability to create playlists that cater to their preferences, which is essential for a personalized listening experience.

4. Transparency and trust issues: Users express concerns about the app's business model, including the perceived nickel-and-diming of premium users.

Supporting evidence: "I already pay for premium but that only allows for 15 hours of audiobooks and now they want me to pay for an add on thats the same price as the monthly fee" (Review 79)

Why it matters: Users want to feel confident in the app's business practices and value for money, which is essential for building trust and loyalty.

5. Discovery pain points: Users struggle to find music they enjoy, with some feeling frustrated by the lack of control over recommendations.

Supporting evidence: "Hiding songs isn't the same. It doesn't solve the problem of a not liked song showing up on playlists" (Review 55)

Why it matters: Users value the ability to discover new music that resonates with them, which is essential for a engaging and enjoyable listening experience.
